# Google Ads Plan (Free-Credit Launch)

## Setup

1. **Create a dedicated Google account in the business's name** (e.g., a Gmail like `contact.contractopsai@gmail.com`) rather than using a personal Google account. This is free and takes a couple of minutes. No Google policy actually bans running business ads from a personal account, but a dedicated account avoids billing-name mismatches that cause friction with invoicing/GST later, and looks cleaner if the account is ever reviewed.
2. **Sign up for Google Ads with that account** and check the **Promotions** tab inside the account for the live new-customer credit offer. Don't plan around a specific dollar figure from a blog post — the exact amount, spend threshold, and redemption window are account- and region-specific and change over time. Confirm the real number before budgeting anything.
3. Typical mechanic (subject to what's actually shown in-account): spend a minimum amount within a set window (commonly ~60 days) to unlock a matching credit, redeemed via a promo code within ~2-3 weeks of the first ad running; the credit itself posts up to ~5 weeks later. Plan spend pacing around whatever the live offer actually says.

## Compliance note

ContractOps AI is a SaaS tool, not a law firm or legal-services provider, and it already carries a "not legal advice" disclaimer. That means it does **not** fall under Google's stricter legal-services advertiser certification requirements — standard SaaS ad review applies. The one real restriction: if any ad creative uses AI-generated voice or video, Google now requires explicit disclosure of that — skip AI-generated ad creative unless it's clearly labeled, to avoid disapproval.

## Campaign structure

- **Single, focused Search campaign** to start — don't spread the free credit across Display/YouTube/Discovery for a first test.
- **High-intent keywords**: "AI contract review tool," "contract risk analyzer," "NDA review AI," "AI contract redlining," "review contract before signing AI." Avoid broad/generic terms ("legal AI," "contract") that burn budget on low-intent clicks.
- **Landing page**: point ads at the redesigned homepage (`/`), which now has a live product preview, a clear "how it works" flow, and pricing — all of which directly improve Quality Score and conversion rate versus the old page.
- **Ad copy**: lead with the free-analysis hook ("1 free contract analysis, no card required") since that's the lowest-friction conversion action already built into the product.

## Budget discipline

- Set a **daily budget cap** low enough that total spend stays inside the free credit's unlock threshold and window — don't let the campaign run unattended at a pace that risks real charges before the credit posts.
- **I will not enable billing, raise budgets, or let real (non-credit) spend occur without asking first, every time** — this follows the standing rule around purchases/spend requiring explicit confirmation, not a one-time approval.
- Pause the campaign manually once the credit is close to exhausted, then decide together whether to continue with real spend based on what the test showed.

## Tracking

- Set up a **Google Ads conversion action** on the meaningful signup event (account creation or first analysis started) before spending anything — without this, there's no way to tell if the campaign is actually working.
- If Google Analytics isn't already on the site, add it at the same time (free) so traffic quality can be judged beyond just ad-platform numbers.

## Sequencing

Run this *after* the SEO basics (`SEO_PLAN.md`) are in place — a page with correct metadata and a submitted sitemap is a stronger, more measurable destination for paid traffic than one without.
